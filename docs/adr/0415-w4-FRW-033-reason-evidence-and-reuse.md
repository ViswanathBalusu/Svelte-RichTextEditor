# 0415. The reason of a decline as evidence, and the copy a stopped workflow offers

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-18. Written while finishing FRW-033 (lane w4). It completes ADR 0386, which left two criteria open. The owner has decided neither point (docs/owner-decisions-2026-11-15.md has no decision on FRW-033). Each item takes the smallest reading that works; the owner may change any of them.

1. **Where the reason of a decline is recorded.** FRW-033 criterion 1 asks that the reason is "recorded in the control commit and the evidence". A control commit holds no personal text: 12 section 7 and ADR 0013 allow only salted hashes of evidence in the chain, and a free-text reason is personal data. The reading taken: the reason is evidence of the participant who declined. It is stored in `workflow_evidence` (item `declined`, with the time and the reason), and one control commit with origin `decline` follows the decline and carries the salted hash of that evidence. So the commit commits to the reason without holding it, and a verifier recomputes the hash from the stored row (`recomputeCommitment`) and finds it in the chain, as for a signing or a completion. The reason stays in the audit entry of the transition (ADR 0386 point 3) as well.
2. **The anchor's origin.** The origin of an evidence anchor was `sign` or `rules`. A third value, `decline`, is added (authority `EvidenceCommitPayload`, the room's anchor check, the outbox, the storage codec). It marks the commit and nothing else.
3. **Network identity.** The decline of a user's own token records the request's address and user agent as the other evidence does, under the tenant's capture setting (FRW-042). A decline made by the host's service credential records none, because the socket is the host's, not the participant's.
4. **A bound on the reason.** A row of evidence is at most 4096 bytes. The reason is 1 to 2000 characters (ADR 0386), which can be 8000 bytes. A reason of more than 3000 bytes of UTF-8 after trimming is refused as `validation_failed` with the pointer `/reason`, before anything changes. 3000 leaves room for the other members of the row.
5. **Void.** A void is the owner's act, not a participant's, and is not evidence of a participant. Its reason stays in the audit entry only. The criterion asks for the reason of a decline only.
6. **What the copy of a stopped workflow starts from.** FRW-033 criterion 5: "the owner wants to reuse the document". The lock banner of a declined or voided workflow offers "Copy to a new document" to a reader who may manage the document, when the host handles the fork request. It raises `de-fork-request` with `atSeq` equal to the workflow's `sentSeq`, so the copy is the document as it was sent (the sent version), without the values the participants had filled in. The host may fork at another `seq`. What a copy carries (text only, no threads, history, workflow state or grants) is owner decision 42.

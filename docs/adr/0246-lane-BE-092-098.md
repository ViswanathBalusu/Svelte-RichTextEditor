# 0246. Lane BE-092 to BE-098: open questions and blockers

**Status:** proposed (2026-11-13). Written by the developer lane BE-092-098, round 1. The owner decides; the spec default is used until then.

## Context

Stories BE-092 to BE-098 in `docs/stories/BE-backend.md` cover storage and export quotas, dedicated deployments, CDM migrations run by an operator, self-hosted upgrades, encrypted backups, the hosted on-call process and projections outside a room (ADR 0019). Round 1 finds that most of them depend on work not yet built.

## Decision 1: concurrency at the export limit (BE-092 criterion 3)

The story asks whether a tenant at its export concurrency limit queues the next export or receives `quota_exceeded`. 06 §12 says that exceeding any quota returns `quota_exceeded`; 09 §9.1 describes a concurrency limit that reads like queueing.

- **Default used (spec default):** `quota_exceeded` with `Retry-After`, as 06 §12 states. No queueing is added for quotas. The queue of BE-049 to BE-051 still orders accepted jobs.
- **Status:** not implemented in round 1. BE-092 is not ticked.
- **Owner question:** confirm `quota_exceeded` for export concurrency, or choose queueing (09 §9.1).

# 0323. Lane U-EXP-097-112: decisions and open questions (round 4)

Status: proposed. Owner decisions stay with the owner; the spec default is used where one exists.

Context: lane U-EXP-097-112 closes the unticked criteria of EXP-097 to EXP-112 that were blocked on a database, a container, a browser or another package. Round 4 reviewed the open criteria of EXP-099, EXP-100, EXP-101, EXP-102, EXP-103, EXP-104, EXP-105, EXP-106 and EXP-107 against the code.

## Findings (round 4)

1. EXP-099 criterion 1 (import runs in a sandboxed worker with no network and no credentials). The importer `importDocx` in `packages/export-docx/src/import.ts` runs in the calling Node process. The asset worker has a Podman sandbox (`services/asset-worker/src/sandbox-podman.ts`), but it accepts images and SVG, not DOCX. Running the importer there needs a new job kind, a container image that carries the package and a change in `services/asset-worker`. Not built in this round; criterion stays unticked.

2. EXP-103 criterion 3 (accept and reject of imported suggestions). A grep of `packages`, `services` and `apps` for accept and reject commands on suggestion marks finds none. The criterion needs the editor's suggestion commands, which do not exist yet. Stays unticked.

3. EXP-104 (regions from editable ranges). The importer reads `w:permStart` and `w:permEnd` as preserved atoms (`import.ts`, label `permission range`, A2). Producing `region` nodes from them changes the A2 design and needs the owner's reading of "imported as locked" (Notes of EXP-104). Not started; no criterion ticked.

4. EXP-105 (Office math). The import keeps `m:oMath` as a preserved `equation` label. The conversion through MathML needs the converter chosen in EXP-054, a spike whose criteria are unticked. Blocked on EXP-054.

5. EXP-101 criterion 2 (nested table) and criterion 4 (floating object placed inline). These conflict with A2: the importer keeps both as preserved content with a warning. The owner decides which reading holds. Criterion 3 (pictures through the asset worker) needs BE-043 and BE-045 integration. Criterion 5 (`seq` field) is not built.

6. EXP-102 criteria 2 to 5 and EXP-107 criterion 4. These depend on the job result of EXP-098 and on the owner's reading of A2 for footnotes, fields and content controls. EXP-098 is outside this lane's round-4 scope (handled earlier).

## Open questions (not decided in this lane)

- Which reading of "imported as locked" (EXP-104) holds, and what policy imported regions get (`inherit` is the spec's stated default for the policy in EXP-104 criterion 3).
- Whether the DOCX importer runs in the asset worker's sandbox or in its own job sandbox (EXP-099 criterion 1). Spec default: a sandboxed worker with no network and no credentials; the place of that worker is not decided.
- Whether a label built from a Word author name can ever match a real tenant user (EXP-103 criterion 2). The authority's rule is not specified.
- The feature `preserve` that an import with preserved atoms needs in an envelope's `requires` (EXP-098 Notes).

## Consequences

No criterion is ticked by this lane in round 4. The Notes of EXP-099, EXP-103, EXP-104 and EXP-105 are updated with the blockers above.

# 0390. Participant tokens: how `wfp` gives fill, and what the room does with it

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-18. Written while building FRW-038 (lane w4). 12 §7 and 05 §3.1 give the claims and leave these open. Each item takes the smallest reading that works; the owner may change any of them. None is an owner decision of 2026-11-15 or 2026-11-17 (decision 5a names the claim of form mode, `wfr`, which this does not change).

1. **`wfp` and `wfr` do not mix.** A token with `wfp` fills only as the participant it binds to. The roles come from that participant's entry in the workflow, never from the token's own fill-roles claim (`wfr` by default, 5a). A token with `wfp` that binds to no one has no fill capability and does not fall back to `wfr`. A token with no `wfp` is unchanged: form mode (12 §13) gives it the roles of `wfr` while the document is not locked.
2. **The room binds, not the token service.** The token service verifies the signature and keeps `sub`, `wfp`, `amr` and `auth_time` on the identity. The binding to a participant needs the workflow, which the room holds and which changes after a token is issued (a draft is sent, a participant is activated, a workflow ends). So the session passes the raw `sub` and `wfp` to the room (`Connection.participantClaim`) and the room runs `bindParticipant` each time it computes access. A token opened while the workflow is still a draft has no fill; once the workflow is sent, the room's next computation gives the participant fill without a new token.
3. **What the binding yields.** `participant` (the identifier), `participantRoles` (that participant's roles) and `participantActive` (`fillActive`: the participant is active in a `sent` or `in_progress` workflow). Binding needs the participant's recorded `sub` to equal the token's `sub`; a participant with no recorded `sub` cannot be bound (ADR 0249 decision 13).
4. **"`access.fill.participant` is true".** On the wire `fill.participant` is the participant identifier (DEP `Access.fill`), so "true" is read as "set to the identifier of the bound participant".
5. **A `perm.changed` for a changed `wfp`.** The room compares access, and the fill part (participant, roles, active, allowed) is in the comparison, so a refresh that changes it sends `perm.changed`. The refresh keeps the rule of 05 §3.1 that `sub` does not change; a person who holds two participants of one workflow can switch between them.
6. **Ended workflows.** A participant of a `declined`, `voided` or `expired` workflow has no `fill` on the wire: the lock of those states sets `workflowActive` false (`lockFor`), which ends the fill allowance, and `fillActive` is false. A token that has not expired opens a channel without fill.
7. **`amr` and `auth_time`.** They are kept on the channel as the token gave them (PRO-015 criterion 3), with no check of their values, and are sent nowhere. Their durable record in the participant's evidence is FRW-040 and FRW-041.
8. **No passcode or identity endpoint.** The only operation with `verify` in its name is `participantVerify`, the check of the participant's access code (FRW-039): the host registers the code, the service stores a hash and compares. The service issues no passcode and checks no identity.

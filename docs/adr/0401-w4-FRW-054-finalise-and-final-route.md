# 0401. Finalising a sealed workflow and reading the sealed PDF

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-18. Written while building FRW-054 (lane w4). 12 §6.2 says `completed` goes to `finalised` when the sealed PDF is stored, and 12 §10 lists `GET /documents/{id}/workflow/final`. They leave open what lock `finalised` has, whether the transition is a control commit when no lock changes, who may read the PDF and what the answer looks like. The owner has not seen this record.

1. **`finalised` keeps the lock of `completed`.** `lockFor('finalised')` is `{ documentState: 'locked', workflowActive: false }` (it was left undecided, FRW-029 Open questions). 12 §6.2 says terminal states keep the lock, the sealed PDF is the record of the document as signed, and an unlocked `finalised` document would let an owner edit a document whose seal no longer matches it. The document state `archived` is not used: 07 §4 gives it other effects (comments) that nobody has asked for here. `packages/authority/test/frw-029.workflow-lock.test.ts` now states that every workflow state has a lock.
2. **The transition is anchored.** `completed` to `finalised` changes no lock, so `recordTransition` would write no grants revision and so no control commit. FRW-030 criterion 1 and FRW-054 criterion 2 name `finalised` among the transitions that are control commits. `recordTransition` has a new option `anchor`; with it, a grants revision is written even when no lock changes, the room records its control commit, and the audit entry `grants.changed` and `workflow.transition` follow as for any transition. Only `finaliseSealed` sets it. The other transitions that change no lock (`sent` to `in_progress`) are unchanged: they still write no control commit.
3. **The seam.** `finaliseSealed` (`services/document-service/src/workflow/finalise-sealed.ts`) takes the sealed bytes, their hash and whether the seal carries a time-stamp, as the signing service will give them (FRW-052, FRW-053). It refuses a seal without a time-stamp before anything is stored, because stored bytes cannot be removed. The port `SealedPdfStore` (`sealed-store.ts`) has `store` and `read` and no replace or delete. `ObjectSealedPdfStore` is the write-once object store and the PostgreSQL record; `MemorySealedPdfStore` serves the memory composition and tests. The PostgreSQL composition (`pgParts`) does not yet create an object store, so `sealedPdfs` is absent there and the route answers `not_found`.
4. **`GET /documents/{id}/workflow/final`.** The capability is the head content's (`read.head`), as for the workflow resource: 07 §12 has no row for it (G95). A stricter rule, such as `manage` or a participant of the workflow, is the owner's to choose; the sealed PDF carries the certificate pages and so personal data. The answer is the PDF itself (`application/pdf`), with its SHA-256 as `Repr-Digest` (RFC 9530) and as the strong `ETag` (`"sha256:<hex>"`), and `If-None-Match` gives 304. No signed URL: reading from the service keeps every read authorised. Before `finalised` the answer is `not_found`. No other method is routed for the path.
5. **The feature `seal` is not announced.** 12 §10 maps the feature to a conformance profile that a server announcing it must pass (FRW-068). The server cannot seal yet, so the route exists and the feature is not announced.
6. **Not done.** The signed, time-stamped chain head written to write-once storage (criterion 3) needs the signing service. No caller reads a sealed PDF from the signing service and calls `finaliseSealed`; the retry of a run that stopped has no stated policy; the operator alert is a flag of the gate and an audit entry, not a delivery.

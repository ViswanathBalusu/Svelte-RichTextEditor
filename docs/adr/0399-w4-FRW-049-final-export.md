# 0399. The final export of a completed workflow

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-18. Written while building FRW-049 (lane w4). 12 §8.2 step 1 and §6.1 name the export and leave the points below open. The owner has not seen this record.

1. **When it starts.** In the `/complete` route (ADR 0398), when the completion raises `completed`. The start is part of the request but does not undo it: a failure to start (no `pdf` target, no `completedSeq`) is audited as `workflow.finalisation_failed` with `stage: "start"` and the workflow stays `completed`. A restart after a crash between the transition and the start is not built (no sweep looks for a `completed` workflow without a job).
2. **`completedSeq` of the workflow.** The highest `completedSeq` among the completed participants. `finalExportRequest` (authority) turns it into the request: `seq`, `fields: flatten`, profile `final`, `certificate` (absent reads as false, ADR 0250 item 2).
3. **The job.** A job of kind `export` for the target `pdf`, as a full export by the service (12 §8.2: the owner's PDF, not a participant's projection; the second review's SR-M3 about a PDF per participant is not decided here), with `options { fields: "flatten", certificate }`, `profile: "final"` and the member `finalisation { workflow, certificate }` that marks it. The content is rebuilt at `completedSeq`. The job identifier is derived from the document, the workflow, the `seq` and the certificate option and the job is queued only if absent, so a repeat starts none. The monthly compile budget and the tenant's export concurrency are not applied: a completed workflow must not be refused its final PDF by a quota. That is a decision to confirm.
4. **Who owns the job.** The actor who sent the workflow, found in the audit entry of the transition to `sent`; the completing actor when the audit has none. The job then belongs to the owner under the rule that an export job belongs to its requester (D69), so `export.progress`, `export.completed` and `export.failed` go to the owner's channel and the owner reads the job (state, error, diagnostics) with `GET /exports/{id}`.
5. **The `completed` version.** Stored at `completedSeq` with kind `completed` (08 §4), once (a second creation for the same `seq` and kind returns the stored one).
6. **Failure.** The job's failure, after its retries, does not change the workflow: it stays `completed` and `finalised` is not raised. The export hooks record `workflow.finalisation_failed` (`stage: "export"`, the job, the `seq` and the error code) for the operator; the host receives `dep.export.failed.v1`; the owner has the notification and the job. No retry is built: a failed job stays failed under its identifier, so a retry needs a decision (a new job identifier or a route to requeue it). Open questions 26 and ADR 0148 name the gap.
7. **Not built here.** The certificate pages themselves (FRW-050: the export worker does not read `options.certificate` before that story), the seal and the store of the sealed PDF and the `finalised` event (FRW-053, FRW-054).

# 0386. Declining and voiding a workflow: who may, what is kept, what follows

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-18. Written while building FRW-033 (lane w4). 12 §6.2 gives the words and leaves these open. Each item takes the smallest reading that works; the owner may change any of them.

1. **Who may decline.** A user whose token binds to the participant: its `wfp` names the participant in the path and whose `sub` is the one recorded for that participant (`bindParticipant`, the rule of FRW-038). The host's service credential may decline for a participant it names (05 §6.1: the service credential acts on every route). The owner and any other user are `forbidden`, as is a participant with no recorded `sub`. A `cc` participant may not decline (`declineWorkflow`). A workflow that is not `in_progress` is `conflict`: a participant of a `sent` workflow who has not opened the document does not decline yet.
2. **Who may void.** A caller with `manage`, from `sent` or `in_progress`. A `correcting` workflow is not voided: it is resumed first. A draft is `conflict`.
3. **The reason.** The body is `{ "reason": text }` of 1 to 2000 characters. A reason that is blank after trimming is `validation_failed` with the pointer `/reason`, and nothing changes. The trimmed reason is kept in the audit entry of the transition (`workflow.transition`, with `by`, the participant who declined, or null for a void) and nowhere else. It is not in the webhook (`participant_declined`, `voided`): the data names the workflow, the document, the `seq` and the chain head, and the participant for a decline (FRW-036). The criterion also asks for the reason in the control commit and in the evidence; a control commit records a grants revision and has no member for a reason, and the evidence record of the workflow is not wired, so those two are not done.
4. **After the stop.** Every participant who has not completed is marked `closed`; one who has completed keeps `completed`. The lock is `locked` with no workflow running (`lockFor`), so the owner's edit, a restore and a replacement of the content are refused, and a participant cannot fill. `declined` and `voided` admit no transition: send, correct, resume, void and decline are all `conflict`.
5. **Reuse.** The host forks the document with its service credential (`POST /documents/{id}/fork`, HIS-022); the copy is a draft with no workflow. The component's `de-fork-request` is HIS-022's.
6. **Method and path.** `POST /documents/{id}/workflow/void` and `POST /documents/{id}/workflow/participants/{pid}/decline`, as the other actions (G95: assumed). Both take an `Idempotency-Key`.

# ADR 0342: Lane U-FRW-055-072, round 4 open questions

Status: proposed (2026-11-13). Owner decisions stay the owner's. Each item uses the spec's default where one exists.

## Context

Round 4 of the unblock lane for FRW-055 to FRW-072 looked for criteria that PostgreSQL, object storage, a container or a browser could prove. Most open criteria in this range are blocked by something the spec leaves undefined or by work outside a single lane. The stories examined this round were FRW-055, FRW-058, FRW-062, FRW-064, FRW-069, FRW-071. No criterion was ticked in this round, because no test could be written that proves one without a decision or a new feature.

## Findings and proposed defaults

1. FRW-055 (evidence bundle). Criteria 3 (commits) and 4 (tombstones still verify) need the log segment and the chain from `@docedit/history`. `@docedit/authority` does not depend on `@docedit/history` (`packages/authority/package.json`). Adding that dependency is a package.json change made through `scripts/pnpm-locked.ps1`, which this lane does not make. Default: keep the bundle's commit half in the history service and let the bundle take a chain verifier as a port. Needs the main session to approve the dependency or the port.
2. FRW-055 criteria 1, 2 and 6 need the sealed PDF, the signature and time-stamp verifier, and the endpoint with its audit. None is built. The capability for `GET /documents/{id}/workflow/evidence` is not in the matrix of 07 §12 (open question 19). Default: the endpoint needs the workflow service and the owner's capability decision.
3. FRW-058 criterion 1 runs the wording check in CI. CI is root configuration and is not changed here. The spec text uses "qualified" as a term in places, so a repository-wide scan would fail on spec text before any product copy is checked. Default: the scan is scoped to product strings and the certificate template until the owner decides the spec's term.
4. FRW-062 (templates) and FRW-063 (bulk sends). The shape of `/templates` and `/bulk-sends`, the row format, the limits and whether grants are copied are not stated (open questions 29 and 6). Nothing can be tested against a spec that does not define the resource. Default: wait for the owner's decision; no shape invented here.
5. FRW-064 (spreadsheet neutralisation). No spreadsheet-format output of field values is specified (open question 30). Default: the story stays `cond`; no output and no neutraliser are added until that output exists.
6. FRW-069 and FRW-070 (fill methods and events). `packages/element` has `mode="fill"` as an accepted attribute, but none of `nextField`, `focusField(key)`, `getFieldValues`, `setFieldValue`, `validateFields` exists. `FillField` in `packages/ui/src/core-api.ts` has no `key`, which `de-field-change` needs. FRW-069 depends on FRW-076, which is not met (the guard in `packages/engine/src/guard.ts` does not check the assignee). Default: build the fill methods after FRW-076 and add `key` to the fill summary through the core port; the element and engine owners make that change.
7. FRW-071 (complete and decline). `packages/protocol/src/definition/frw.ts` defines the participant `complete` and `decline` actions, but `services/document-service/src` serves neither route. The workflow service behind them is not built. Default: the methods wait for the participant endpoints; the `de-sign-request` payload and the `de-workflow-complete` rule remain open (open question 33).

## Decision requested

Owner, or the main session, to decide items 1 (dependency or port for the bundle chain), 3 (the scope of the wording scan) and 4 (the resource shapes of templates and bulk sends). Items 2, 5, 6 and 7 follow from features that are not yet built.

## Round 5 (2026-11-13)

Stories examined: FRW-056, FRW-057, FRW-059, FRW-062, FRW-063, FRW-064, FRW-068, FRW-070. The earlier rounds' items above still hold.

8. FRW-056 criterion 5 (renewal idempotence). The job queue in PostgreSQL (`services/document-service/src/control-plane/jobs/pg.ts`) enqueues with `ifAbsent` on a primary key. `services/document-service/test/control-plane/frw-056.renewal-queue.pg.test.ts` runs against PostgreSQL in Podman and shows one renewal per document and expiry. The renewal itself is not wired to the queue. Default: keep the criterion open until the workflow service enqueues renewals with that key.
9. FRW-057 criterion 3 (schema). `layout.json` has no schema in `spec/`, and the export that writes it is not built, so "validated against its schema before use" has nothing to validate against. The rectangle clause stays as tested in `packages/authority`. Default: the schema is the export's to define (09 §9.3); the owner or the export owner should publish it into `spec/`.
10. FRW-059 criteria 1 to 3 need the `SignatureProvider` port in 05 §8.3 (a PRO change) and the workflow service's evidence tables. Default: no change here.
11. FRW-062, FRW-063 and FRW-064: unchanged from item 4 and item 5.
12. FRW-068 needs a conforming server for the `workflow`, `sign` and `seal` profiles, as the story's Notes say. Default: no profile written.

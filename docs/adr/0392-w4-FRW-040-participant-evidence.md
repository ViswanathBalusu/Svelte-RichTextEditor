# 0392. Participant evidence: the first view, taken from the connection

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-18. Written while building FRW-040 (lane w4). 12 §7 lists the evidence and says nothing about where each item is taken. Each item takes the smallest reading that works; the owner may change any of them. None is an owner decision of 2026-11-15 or 2026-11-17.

1. **The first view is recorded when the channel opens.** A participant's token with `wfp` opens a channel on the real data plane; the session tells the workflow service (`DataPlaneDeps.participantOpened`, after the channel is open, never delaying it). The service binds the token to a participant (`bindParticipant`, FRW-038), stores the first view (`recordFirstView`, FRW-031: the time, and `sent` to `in_progress`), records the evidence and raises `participant_viewed`. A repeated open, a token that binds to no one, a draft and a workflow that is not running do nothing. A failure of the hook is reported and the channel stays open; if the view could not be stored (a concurrent transition), the next open tries again, because the first view is not yet on record.
2. **Where each fact comes from.** The session: the id of the DEP session of the connection. The address: the remote address of the socket as the service sees it; a forwarded-for header is not read, because it is the client's word, and a deployment behind a proxy needs a trusted-proxy setting that is not decided here. The user agent: the `User-Agent` header of the upgrade request, cut to 512 characters (it is the browser's own header; no message of the protocol carries one). The time: the service clock at the open. `amr`: as the token gave it, unchecked, and left out when the token has none. `auth_time` is not recorded as evidence: 12 §7 lists `amr` only.
3. **What a view row holds.** `session`, `viewedAtMs` and, when given, `amr` as items, and the address and the agent under the tenant's capture setting (FRW-042). The row's participant is the workflow's participant identifier; the audit entry of the transition has the user's surrogate.
4. **Only what applies.** `EvidenceLedger.record` takes the participant's kind and refuses, with a `RangeError`, any item that does not apply to it (`applicableItems`) and any member that is no item of the record. An approver has no consent, signing or adoption item; a `cc` has no completion. The write path invents nothing and keeps nothing extra.
5. **A view moves the workflow.** The first view of anyone moves a `sent` workflow to `in_progress` (FRW-031); the tests of FRW-033, FRW-034 and FRW-038 that connect a participant before a transition now start from `in_progress`.
6. **Not built.** The consent, the signing and the completion events are recorded by routes that do not exist yet (FRW-044, FRW-046, FRW-047), so their evidence, the signature image hash from the stored asset (criterion 3) and one event for each of several signings (criterion 5) are not recorded; the builders are the pure functions of `participant-evidence.ts`.

# ADR 0249: Lane FRW-027-039 decisions (workflow send, order, decline and void)

**Status:** proposed (maintainer's proposal; the owner has not reviewed it)
**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Stories:** FRW-027, FRW-031, FRW-033 (criteria ticked); FRW-028 to FRW-030, FRW-032, FRW-034 to FRW-039 (not started in this lane)

## Context

12 §6.1 and §6.2 define the workflow resource and its states, but leave several points open. The
pure parts of FRW-027, FRW-031 and FRW-033 are built in `packages/authority/src/workflow/`
(`lifecycle.ts`, `order.ts`, `send.ts`), with tests in `packages/authority/test/workflow.*.test.ts`.
The service (endpoints, idempotency, control commits, webhooks, `wf.changed`) is not built. Each
decision below was taken where the spec is silent, and is the spec's default or the reading stated in
the story.

## Decisions

1. **Must-act participants.** Kinds `filler`, `signer` and `approver` must act. Kind `cc` never acts,
   never fills and does not count as "the last participant". (FRW-031 notes give this reading.)
2. **Activation.** The active participants are the must-act participants who have not completed and
   whose `order` is the lowest among them. Equal orders are active together.
3. **Fill access.** A participant may fill only in states `sent` and `in_progress` and when active.
   This is the derived `access.fill.active` of FRW-031 criterion 1.
4. **Send checklist.** A `required` field with no assignee, a `role_def` with no participant, a rule
   set that does not compile (`compileDocument`), and an unresolved `ins`, `del` or `fmt` mark each
   produce one problem that lists every failure of that kind. The suggestion problem uses the
   registry code `pending_suggestions`. The other problem codes (`required_field_unassigned`,
   `role_without_participant`, `rules_invalid`) are local names; the registry has no code for them.
   The service decides the HTTP mapping (`validation_failed` is the likely one).
5. **Send refusals.** A caller without `manage` is refused as `forbidden`, then a record that is not
   `draft` as `invalid_state`, then a failed checklist as `checklist_failed`. The check order is the
   reading of FRW-027 criterion 6 and criterion 5.
6. **Decline.** Only from `in_progress` (12 §6.2 diagram has no decline from `sent`). The reason is
   required; a reason that is empty after trimming is no reason, and the stored reason is trimmed. Only
   a participant who must act may decline (a `cc` participant or an unknown identifier is `forbidden`).
7. **Void.** A caller with `manage` only, from `sent` or `in_progress` only, with a reason as in
   decision 6. The check order is caller, then state, then reason.
8. **Stopped workflows.** `declined` and `voided` admit no transition in these functions. Whether
   `expired`, `completed` and `finalised` are terminal for the lock is FRW-029's open question and is
   not decided here.
9. **Completion.** The last must-act participant to complete moves an `in_progress` workflow to
   `completed`. A completion is refused as `forbidden` for a participant who is not active and as
   `invalid_state` outside `in_progress`.
10. **Registry codes of refusals** (added by the review fixer, 2026-11-13; `workflow/problems.ts`).
    `forbidden` stays `forbidden` (403). `invalid_state` is `conflict` (409), as the service already
    answers a request that conflicts with a resource's state. `reason_required` is
    `validation_failed` (400). A failed checklist is one problem that lists every failure; each
    failure carries its own registry code, `pending_suggestions` for unresolved suggestions and
    `validation_failed` for the other three checks. The problem's code is `pending_suggestions` when
    that is the only failing check, and `validation_failed` otherwise. The local check names stay as
    the failure's `code`, so a client can tell the checks apart.
11. **Lock of a workflow state** (round 2, 2026-11-13; `workflow/lock.ts`, FRW-029). `sent` and
    `in_progress` lock the document with fill kept for the active participants (`documentState`
    `locked`, `workflowActive` true). `declined` and `voided` lock it with `workflowActive` false
    (FRW-029 criterion 5). `draft` is active. `correcting`, `completed`, `finalised` and `expired` are
    not decided by 12, so `lockFor` returns null for them: no default is taken. Finding (not fixed
    here, for SYN and PRM): room step 1 admits any connection whose `fill.allowed` is true, and the
    owner and editor roles carry `fill`, so their non-fill commits under the lock are refused with
    `permission_denied` where 12 §3.3 names `read_only` for the document state. A viewer gets
    `read_only`.
12. **Webhook data** (round 2, FRW-036; `workflow/events.ts`). The data holds the workflow, document,
    `seq` and chain head, and the participant where the event concerns one. It is built field by field
    and never copies a contact detail or an IP address. The event types are `dep.workflow.` plus the
    name of 12 §10. Raising and delivering the events is the service's (FRW-030, FRW-036).
13. **Token binding** (round 2, FRW-038; `workflow/binding.ts`). A token binds to the participant
    named by its `wfp` claim only when the participant has a recorded `sub` equal to the token's `sub`.
    A participant with no recorded `sub` cannot be bound (reading of FRW-038 criterion 2, proposed).
    `sub` is an optional member of `WorkflowParticipant` (12 §6.1 names it). Fill from a bound token
    needs the workflow to be `sent` or `in_progress` and the participant active.
15. **Correction and resume** (round 3, FRW-032; `workflow/correction.ts`). `correct` needs `manage`
    and is allowed from `in_progress` only; `resume` needs `manage` and is allowed from `correcting`
    only. A paused participant is one whose fill is not active while `correcting`. On `resume`, a
    changed body resets every completed participant; a changed field resets the completed participant
    who owns it; a changed field with no owner counts as a body change (the conservative reading). A
    reset clears `completed` only. Earlier signature values and evidence are not touched (12 does not
    say what happens to them; Open questions). Proposed.
16. **Workflow view privacy** (round 3, FRW-035; `workflow/state-view.ts`). The object sent on
    `ch.opened` and in `wf.changed` carries the state, the send position, the lock, and only the
    connection's own participant (identifier, kind and a one-word status). It carries no other
    participant's entry, `sub` or roles. Reading of the privacy question in FRW-035 Notes: no
    participant learns another participant's details through the push. Proposed; the owner decides.
17. **Deadline and reminders** (round 3, FRW-034; `workflow/schedule.ts`). A deadline is read as UTC:
    a `Z` suffix or no suffix; any other offset, and any impossible calendar value, is refused. Reminders
    are raised only in `sent` and `in_progress`. The anchor of `firstAfterDays` (send or activation) is
    a parameter the caller supplies, with no default. Reminder numbers are computed from the instant and
    the count already raised, so each is raised once per interval. Proposed.

## Round 3 scope

Round 3 (FRW-032, FRW-034, FRW-035) adds pure functions and tests only. No endpoint, control commit,
event, webhook, scheduler or channel push is built in this lane, so no criterion of these stories is
ticked. Decisions 15 to 17 are the spec defaults or the readings stated above.

## Not decided here (round 3 addition)

- The state `correcting` for reminders (decision 17 treats it as no reminder) and for the lock
  (`lockFor` returns null; FRW-029 Open questions).
14. **Not decided in round 2.** The access-code rate limit and its figures (FRW-039): 12 gives no
    numbers, and the hash of a code needs a memory-hard function that the universal packages cannot
    call (only SHA-256 is in the platform helpers). Left to the service and to the owner. The
    reminder schedule and the expiry (FRW-034): the state and the schedule are not built, and the
    server clock is the service's.

## Not decided here

- Whether the approver's completion skips field checks (FRW-031 criterion 5; FRW-047).
- Whether a decline or void from `correcting` is allowed (FRW-032, open question).
- The first-viewed time and the events `participant_viewed`, `participant_activated`, `voided`,
  `participant_declined` (FRW-031 criterion 4, FRW-033 criteria 1 and 2; FRW-036).
- The idempotency key of `send` (FRW-027 criterion 5).
- The lock of the document and the `sent` version (FRW-027 criterion 3; FRW-029).

## Consequences

The pure functions can be called by the workflow service as they are. They do not touch the record
they are given: a refusal returns the refusal, and the caller keeps its record.

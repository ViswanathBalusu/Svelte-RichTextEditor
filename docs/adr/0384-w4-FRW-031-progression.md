# 0384. How a sent workflow progresses: first view, completion, the lock of `completed`, the push

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-17. Written while building FRW-031 and the service side of FRW-035 (lane w4). 12 §6 leaves these open. Each item takes the smallest reading that works; the owner may change any of them.

1. **The lock of `completed`.** `lockFor('completed')` is `{ documentState: 'locked', workflowActive: false }`: the document stays locked and nobody fills it any more. 12 §6.2 says terminal states keep the lock; `completed` is the end of the participants' work and the body must stay frozen for the final PDF and the seal (FRW-049, FRW-054). `correcting`, `finalised` and `expired` stay undecided (FRW-029 Notes). Before this change `completed` had no lock, so the last completion could not be recorded.
2. **First view.** The first time any participant opens the document while the workflow is `sent`, it moves to `in_progress` (`openWorkflow`). Each participant's first view is stored once, as `firstViewedAt` (ISO 8601 UTC) on that participant of the resource, and raises `participant_viewed` once. The member is added to the `Workflow` schema of the protocol (`participants[].firstViewedAt`, optional). A later view stores and raises nothing. A view of a workflow that is not `sent` or `in_progress` is refused (`invalid_state`). A view in `in_progress` is stored as a transition to the same state, so it is audited as `workflow.transition` like any other write.
3. **Completion.** `recordCompletion` completes an active participant. The activation marks of the resource (`active`, `pending`, `completed`; ADR 0383, point 6) are rewritten for every participant. A participant who is not active (an earlier order first, a `cc`, one who has completed already) is `forbidden`; a workflow that is not `in_progress` is `invalid_state`. The workflow moves to `completed` when no participant who must act is left (the reading of ADR 0249). An approver completes with no field checks. The checks, the `rules` commit and the route belong to FRW-047, which calls this function; `completedSeq` is passed in by it.
4. **Events.** `recordFirstView` and `recordCompletion` return the events to raise (`participant_viewed`; `participant_completed`, one `participant_activated` for each participant activated, and `completed`); the route that has the request context raises them with `workflowEvent`.
5. **The push.** `recordTransition` tells the room once, after the state moves, with the new workflow record and the permission state (`RoomNotifier.workflowChanged`, optional). A notifier without it is told of the lock at the old point, as before. The room sends `perm.changed` and then `wf.changed`. When the permission revision is not newer than the room's, `perm.changed` goes out at once (as at an expiry) because no control commit follows it. A room reads the workflow of its document when it starts, unless it is a draft, so `ch.opened` carries it.
6. **Not done.** The data plane does not map the token's `wfp` claim to the connection's `participant`, so a connection's own participant status and the derivation of `access.fill.active` from the record are not live in the service (the room has them, FRW-035 tests). Nothing calls `recordFirstView` when a channel opens, and no route calls `recordCompletion` (FRW-047).

# 0389. Expiry at the deadline and reminders: what 12 leaves open

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-18. Written while building FRW-034 (lane w4). 12 §6.1 and §6.2 give the words `deadline`, `reminders` and `remind`. Each item takes the smallest reading that works; the owner may change any of them. None is an owner decision of 2026-11-15 or 2026-11-17.

1. **Where the reminders count from.** From the instant the participant became active (`activatedAt`), not from the send. A participant of a later order is not asked to act before their turn, so a reminder before it would be a message about nothing. `recordTransition` stamps `activatedAt` (server time, ISO 8601 UTC) for every participant who is active after a transition and was not before; a participant activated again after a correction gets a new time and the count starts again. ADR 0249 decision 17 left the anchor to the caller; this is the caller's choice.
2. **What a reminder is.** `reminder_due` for one participant, with the workflow, document and participant identifiers, the `seq` and the chain head (FRW-036). It is raised for a participant who must act (not a `cc`), is active and has not completed, while the workflow is `sent` or `in_progress`. A `correcting` workflow raises none (its participants are paused).
3. **Both timers or none.** `reminders` with only one of `firstAfterDays` and `everyDays` raises nothing: 12 gives no default for the missing one.
4. **A backlog is one reminder.** When several reminders are due at one run (the service was stopped, or the run was late), one event is raised and the count moves to the latest due number. The host gets one message, not three.
5. **Once per interval.** The number of the last reminder raised for each activation is kept in the table `workflow_reminders` (migration 0076; `ReminderStore`), not in the workflow resource, because the resource's revision belongs to transitions. The count moves by a compare and set before the event is raised, so of two instances (or a run after a restart) one raises it. If the process stops between the count and the event, that reminder is lost: delivery of an event is at least once only after it is raised, and the schedule prefers a missed reminder to a repeated one. The next interval raises the next.
6. **The deadline.** `deadline` is the instant of 12 §6.1, written with a `Z` (the body check requires it) and compared with the server clock (`deps.now`). A client time or a time zone of the workflow does not enter. A `sent` or `in_progress` workflow whose deadline has passed becomes `expired` through `recordTransition`: a lock, a control commit, the stored state, the push to the room (`wf.changed`) and the audit entry (`workflow.transition`, `by` null, `cause` `deadline`, actor `system`). Every participant who has not completed is marked `closed`. A `correcting` workflow does not expire: 12 draws no arrow from it and its owner is editing.
7. **The lock of `expired`.** `lockFor('expired')` was undecided (FRW-029 Open questions). It is now `locked` with `workflowActive` false, as for `declined`, `voided` and `completed` (12 §6.2: terminal states keep the lock). `finalised` stays undecided. A participant of an expired workflow cannot fill: the room gets the new permission state with the transition.
8. **When the schedule runs.** `workflow.schedule`, a scheduled task of the control plane's scheduler (BE-052), every minute for each tenant. The scheduler gives each slot to one worker; the compare and set and the conditional state write are what hold when two run. A deadline is therefore met within a minute and a little more under load; this is a design value, not a measurement.
9. **`remind`.** `POST /documents/{id}/workflow/remind` (method and path assumed, G95), `manage`, no body. It raises `reminder_due` for the active participants who have not completed, changes nothing stored, and does not move the schedule's count. A draft or an ended workflow is `conflict`. Its events carry the `seq` and chain head at the request.
10. **No personal data.** The events carry identifiers only. The host holds the contact details and sends the message (12 §6.3).

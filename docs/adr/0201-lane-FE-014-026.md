# ADR 0201: decisions of lane FE-014-026 (round 2)

**Status:** proposed. **Date:** 2026-11-13. **Stories:** FE-017 (criteria 1, 3 and 5 ticked; 2 and 4 open), FE-023 (note only).

## Context

FE-017 asks for a per-feature list of the Trusted Types policy names and CSP directives a host must
allow, in the integration guide. The guide itself is UX-090 (phase 1b, not yet written). The spike
S2 report, section 8, already holds the measured per-feature list.

## Decisions taken (spec default, to be confirmed)

1. **Where the guide section lives.** `packages/element/docs/policies-and-side-effects.md`, until
   UX-090 places the integration guide. Default taken: keep it next to the code it describes.
2. **Fonts.** The guide's fonts entry lists `font-src` only: the FE-061 registration needs no other
   directive. It is on the page's path: `packages/element/src/session.ts` calls `registerManifestFonts`
   (`font-loader.ts`) once the backend is connected and has a `fonts` port, and each manifest file is
   added to `document.fonts` under a `de-font-` hash alias (`FONT_ALIAS_PREFIX` in
   `font-registration.ts`). The guide lists these aliases as a global side effect, and states that
   they stay after the element is removed. (Corrected in the round 2 fix: the first version said the
   registration had no caller.)
3. **Unmeasured cases stay marked as unmeasured.** The production PDF viewer frame (`pdf/viewer-frame.ts`)
   and the in-page PDF panel without `docedit-worker` are described as not measured. FE-017 criterion 4
   stays open until they are run.
4. **Criterion 2 (CI on an unlisted violation)** is FE-125's browser matrix. It is not wired in this
   round; the name drift check is `packages/element/test/published-policies.test.ts` and the new
   `packages/element/test/integration-policies.test.ts`.
5. **FE-023 criterion 3.** The `$state` scan refuses a non-raw `$state` whose name, type or initialiser
   says "snapshot". A snapshot under another name would pass. Default taken: the criterion stays open
   until the shell has a view snapshot to check.

## Consequences

- FE-017 criteria 2 and 4 remain unticked, with reasons in the story's Notes.
- The guide section must be kept in step with `packages/element/e2e/s2-features.spec.ts` (`GUIDE`) and
  `packages/element/src/trusted-types.ts` (`POLICY_NAMES`); the two test files fail on drift in names.
- The guide's side-effect list must also be kept in step with `FONT_ALIAS_PREFIX`, the session's
  call of `registerManifestFonts` and the `selectionchange` listener of the pinned prosemirror-view;
  `integration-policies.test.ts` reads all three and fails on drift.

## Round 3 (2026-11-13): FE-019 criterion 3

**Stories:** FE-019 (criterion 3 partly; not ticked).

6. **Forced-colours cue for decorations.** Default taken: a find match gets `outline: 1px dashed
   Highlight` and the current match `outline: 3px solid Highlight`, in the forced-colours block of
   `packages/element/src/styles/element.css`. The spec does not give the cue; the story's wording
   ("outline, underline or border") fixes the kind, the widths and styles are this lane's choice and
   are not measured. Owner or UX may change them.
7. **Scope of criterion 3.** Comment ranges and suggestions are not ticked because their decoration
   classes do not exist (FE-046, FE-048 unbuilt). The test is a source check of the stylesheet, not a
   computed-style run.

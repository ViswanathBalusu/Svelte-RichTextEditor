# ADR 0315 (lane V-PRO-103-119): proposed decisions for the protocol unblock lane

Status: proposed. The owner decides the open items; the lane only records what it chose by default.

## Context

Lane V-PRO-103-119, round 1, closes the criteria of PRO-103 to PRO-119 that need a real environment or a second package. Most of that range is blocked by features that do not exist yet (the authority service PRO-103 to PRO-107, the history routes for PRO-119, lease routes for PRO-109 and PRO-111, fault hooks for PRO-111 and PRO-113 and PRO-126). PRO-114 criterion 1 was the one story this round could move.

## Decision 1: bound for a "short-lived" signed asset URL (PRO-114 criterion 1)

- Spec: 10 §3.7 and 05 §6.2 say "short-lived" and give no number.
- The reference service signs asset URLs with `DOCEDIT_SIGNED_URL_SECONDS` (default 300 s, `services/document-service/src/config.ts`). The value is not announced in discovery, so a kit cannot read it.
- Default taken by the kit: an upper bound of 15 minutes, the maximum that 05 §3 sets for tokens, applied to signed URLs by analogy. The constant is `SHORT_LIVED_MS` in `packages/tck/src/profiles/jobs.ts`.
- Owner question: should the spec name a URL lifetime, and should discovery announce it so that the kit can check the service's own value?

## Decision 2: token refresh before a wait in the assets scenario

- The test-mode token lives 60 s (`TEST_TIMERS.tokenLifetimeS`, open question of PRO-109, decision 4 of ADR 0315 U-PRO-103-119). A `ready` check in a container under load can outlast it, so the scenario refreshes the token with `auth.refresh` (05 §3.1) before it waits. This is a kit change, not a change of the 60 s value.

## Decision 3: feature gating of the authorisation rows (PRO-115 criterion 1, round 3)

- Spec: 05 §1 rule 4 and §11. The kit calls a matrix row only when the target announces the feature it needs (`comments`, `history`, `leases`, `assets`, `exports`), and skips it with the reason otherwise.
- The reference service serves the routes of threads, named versions, region restore and leases (`thread-routes.ts`, `versions-routes.ts`, `restore-route.ts`, `lease-routes.ts`), but announces none of `comments`, `history` or `leases` by default (`DEFAULT_FEATURES`, `services/document-service/src/data-plane/settings.ts`).
- Default taken by the lane: the kit keeps gating on announced features. The reference run uses its default features. A second run, in `packages/tck/test/pro-115-authz.reference.test.ts`, sets `DOCEDIT_FEATURES` to include the three features; it shows the routes refuse and allow as the matrix says (rows 4, 5, 8, 11 pass). It does not change what the default service announces.
- Owner question: should the reference service announce `comments`, `history` and `leases` once its routes are complete, and should the kit run rows 4, 5, 8 and 11 in its default run?

## Decision 4: region restore is refused to an editor (PRO-115 row 9; a defect, not a choice)

- Row 9 of 07 §12 needs `edit` in the region. The reference restore route applies the whole-document `manage` guard before it reads `region`, and it does not offer region restore (HIS-018) or dry runs (HIS-021).
- The lane does not change the route. The scenario `authz.restore.region` expects the editor side to fail with `needs manage` until HIS-018 lands, and the test in `pro-115-authz.reference.test.ts` checks for that known failure.

## Not decided here

- PRO-109 criteria 2 and 3 ("within seconds"): the 60 s token lifetime conflicts with the story's wording. Still the owner's (see ADR 0315 U-PRO-103-119, decision 4).
- PRO-110 criterion 5 and PRO-111 criteria 3 and 4 need replay of map and rebase fixtures through the kit, and fault hooks. Not built.
- PRO-116 criterion 4 (one-hour fuzz budget) is a measurement on the reference machine. Not run.

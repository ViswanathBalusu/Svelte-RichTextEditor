# ADR 0226: Decisions in lane EXP-066-078 (export, round 3)

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-13. Written by the developer of lane EXP-066-078 (round 1 of the run). The project owner has not reviewed it. Where a story left a choice open, the choice below is the spec's default or is marked as open.

**Context.** Lane EXP-066-078 covers the export stories EXP-066 to EXP-078 in `docs/stories/EXP-export.md`. Most of them need Podman, the image, a second worker, or a control-plane route owned by another package. This record covers the choices the lane met.

## Decisions

1. **EXP-068 criterion 5 (security notes) is ticked on the notes as written.** The notes are the `## Security notes` section of `services/export-worker/README.md`, checked by `services/export-worker/src/security-notes.test.ts`. The section states the spec's wording of 09 §9.3 item 2: any TeX injection is arbitrary Lua, and the container is the boundary. The file name and heading are the lane's choice; the owner may prefer a document in `docs/` or the security docs of SEC, and the wording can move without changing the criterion.

2. **EXP-069 (sandbox technology) is not decided here.** Its criteria stay unticked.
   - Criterion 1 needs the measurements of gVisor (`runsc`) and of a Firecracker microVM on the reference worker, with the S4 measurements as the baseline. Only the Podman measurements exist (`services/export-worker/out/measure/`). No measurement of gVisor or Firecracker has been taken, and none is invented here.
   - Criterion 2 is answered for gVisor in ADR 0045 (the property list of EXP-070, each with its mechanism). It is not answered for Firecracker. Nothing is checked on a running sandbox.
   - Criterion 3 links from ADR 0007, which is accepted and owned by the project owner. The link is not added here. The owner adds it when the choice is made.
   - The spec default in 09 §9.3 item 3 names both options and no choice. ADR 0045 recommends gVisor for production, with Podman and crun for development. That recommendation is the proposal this lane leaves to the owner; it is not a decision.

3. **EXP-066 (raw compile logs) is not built in this lane.** The raw log is returned as a signed URL inside the job record by `services/document-service/src/control-plane/export-routes.ts` (`exportJobOf`, `ownJob`). That file is owned by the control plane, not by `services/export-worker` or `packages/export-latex`, so it is not changed here. The gap against the criteria is recorded in the story's Notes: a caller without `edit` gets the job without a log URL rather than a `forbidden` refusal (criterion 2); the `text/plain` type with `nosniff` (criterion 3) and the expiry with the artefacts (criterion 4) are the storage's, not checked here. The worker does write the log as `text/plain; charset=utf-8` (`services/export-worker/src/job.ts`); nothing in this lane sets `X-Content-Type-Options`.

4. **EXP-076 criterion 4 stays open, as decided in ADR 0116 decision 4.** The manifest records the local image ID and the class version. The criterion names the image digest. Which one the owner wants is open. This lane does not decide it.

5. **EXP-079 (two independent compiles before sealing) is not started.** It depends on the sealing of FRW-049 and SPK-021, neither of which is built, and on two independent workers. Its criteria 1, 3 and 4 cannot be checked in one export worker. Nothing is ticked.

## Not done in this lane

- EXP-067 criterion 1 (the exports resource schema) and criterion 4 (a worker's proof that the service queued a bundle): the control plane's, and open in ADR 0116 decision 5.
- EXP-070 criteria 2 and 3, EXP-071 criteria 2 and 3, EXP-072 criteria 1, 2 and 4, EXP-068 criteria 2 to 4, EXP-075 and EXP-073: need Podman, the image, or the upload location of EXP-072.
- EXP-076 criteria 2 and 3: several runs on different hosts under Podman.
- EXP-077 (scaling): the autoscaler is in `services/document-service` (BE), and the orchestrators are not set up.
- EXP-078 (upgrade gate): needs the corpus compare of EXP-114 and a deployed image.

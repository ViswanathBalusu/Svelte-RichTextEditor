# ADR 0352: Lane U-CDM-001-027, decisions on the CDM stories 001 to 027

**Status:** proposed (2026-11-13). The owner has not reviewed it.

## Context

Round 2 of the lane closes criteria that earlier rounds left open because they needed a real
environment or another package. In the range CDM-001 to CDM-027, the open criteria are these:

- CDM-002 (last criterion) and CDM-003 (last criterion): wait for the owner to accept ADR 0018 and to
  update 03 §2 and 03 §4 and §5. Owner decisions; not closed by this lane.
- CDM-005 criteria 4 and 5: wait for the review of the layout fixtures by the FE and EXP owners, and
  for the owner's acceptance of ADR 0032 and the update of 03 §7. Human reviews; not closed.
- CDM-011 criterion 3 (CI run) and criterion 5 (kit format). Criterion 5 is closed here (section 1).
- CDM-013 criterion 7: a measurement on the reference machine that the performance target names
  (04 §12). It needs the FND-007 harness and that machine; not measured by this lane.

No open criterion in the range needed PostgreSQL, object storage or a browser run.

## Decisions

1. **Kit format for the canonical-hash fixtures (CDM-011 criterion 5).** The spec does not define the
   format of the conformance kit's copy of the canonical set. The lane takes the spec default by
   precedent: the kit copies the published set byte for byte into `packages/tck/fixtures/canonical/`,
   as it already does for the rules fixtures (PRO-123, `packages/tck/scripts/rules-copy.ts`). The
   layout is the model's own (`index.json`, `inputs/`, `expected/`). The owner of the kit (ADR 0042)
   may choose another format.

## Consequences

- `packages/tck/scripts/canonical-copy.ts` and `packages/tck/test/canonical-copy.test.ts` are new;
  `packages/tck/scripts/generate.ts` calls the copy.
- The kit's copy must be regenerated after every change to `packages/model/fixtures/canonical/`.
- CDM-013 criterion 7 stays open until a measurement on the reference machine exists.

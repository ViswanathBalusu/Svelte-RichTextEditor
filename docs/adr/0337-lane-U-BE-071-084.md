Status: proposed

## BE-076 criterion 2: resume with inflight after the head did not move

The test in `services/document-service/src/data-plane/crash-recovery.test.ts` ("a commit in flight at the kill") resumes a client with `inflight` and expects `sync.caughtUp` when nothing was stored. The reconnect still answers `catchUp` `reload` in that case. The cause was looked at but not settled:

- A resume `view` token that the client holds at head 1 is not the token the room issues at head 0, because the token names the head it was computed at (`v1.*`, `asOfSeq`).
- Passing the token from a channel opened at head 1 (on a second client) still gives `reload`.
- `catchUpFor` in `packages/authority/src/room/room.ts` returns `reload` for `seq < baseSeq`, for a `seq` outside the visibility range, and for snapshot commits after `seq`. Which of these applies after a restart was not shown.

Default taken: none. The test keeps its existing expectation (`reload`). Criterion 2 stays unticked. Owner or authority owner to decide whether a client that resumes at the head should get `stream` with `sync.caughtUp`, and whether the view token should survive a head change.

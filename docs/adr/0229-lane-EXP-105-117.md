# ADR 0229: Lane EXP-105-117, round 1 decisions

Status: proposed (2026-11-13). Decisions here are spec defaults chosen by the lane, not owner decisions.

## Context

Lane EXP-105-117 handles the DOCX import stories EXP-105 to EXP-110, EXP-116 and EXP-117, and the 1b export stories EXP-111 to EXP-115, in the packages `export-docx` and `export-latex`.

## Decision 1: the formatting warning uses the spec's wording

EXP-117 criterion 5 says each preserved kind is reported as "preserved, not editable", with a count. The importer's warning for formatting the editor does not show (`src/import.ts`, two call sites) had the wording "kept with the paragraph and written back to Word; not shown or editable in the editor". The message now begins with "preserved, not editable: " and keeps the rest of the sentence, with "not shown in the editor" in place of "not shown or editable in the editor". The action (`preserved`) and the kind name are unchanged.

Reason: the criterion names the wording; no other kind in the report differed.

## Decision 2: criterion 1 of EXP-117 is checked for order only

The test in `packages/export-docx/test/exp-117-report.test.ts` checks that body atoms keep their document order in the source part. It does not check which kinds are covered. The criterion stays open.

## Not decided here

- EXP-105 needs the converter chosen in EXP-054 (not built); the criteria stay open.
- EXP-106 criterion 3, EXP-107 criterion 4, EXP-108 criteria 2 to 4, EXP-116 criterion 5 and EXP-109 criterion 2 (second clause) wait for the import job of EXP-098.
- EXP-116 criterion 3 (identity-bound values in the document) fails for `docx4j/cr043-tracked.docx`. A fix needs the restoration of identity values on write, which is a design decision for the owner.
- EXP-111 to EXP-115 need a LuaLaTeX compile, veraPDF or a job result from the export service. None is ticked.

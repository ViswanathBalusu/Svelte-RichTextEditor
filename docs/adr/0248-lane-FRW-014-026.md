# ADR 0248: Participant list checks and order reading (lane FRW-014-026)

Status: proposed (a maintainer's proposal from lane FRW-014-026, 2026-11-13; not reviewed by the owner).

## Context

FRW-025 (the workflow resource) and FRW-026 (binding people to roles and ordering them) describe a
participant list that a host or an owner writes. 12 section 6.1 gives the kinds (`filler`, `signer`,
`approver`, `cc`) and the reading of `order` (equal values run in parallel, ascending values in
sequence), but it does not give the type of `order`, whether a participant needs a role, or which
members a writer may give. The endpoint and the published JSON Schema are not built, so the checks
below are pure functions that the endpoint will call.

## Decisions

1. `order` is a safe integer of at least 1. 12 gives no type and no range; the lowest order is 1 in
   every example of 12 section 6.1.
2. A participant holds one or more roles, each a non-empty string, with no role twice (FRW-026
   criterion 1 says "one or more roles").
3. Only the members `id`, `roles`, `kind`, `order`, `sub` and `name` may be written. Any other member,
   including a contact detail such as an e-mail address or a telephone number, is refused as an
   unknown member (FRW-026 criterion 4: the participant holds no contact detail). `sub` and `name`
   are the only identifying members (12 section 6.1).
4. `completed` is not accepted from a writer. Completion is the workflow's own transition
   (`completeParticipant` in `packages/authority/src/workflow/order.ts`).
5. Every problem in a list is reported, each with its index and member, so a writer can fix them all at
   once. The `index` of a list that is not an array is -1.
6. A role is held when at least one participant holds it. Two participants may hold the same role; either
   may then fill that role's fields (12 section 7, FRW-026 criterion 3). The field access that results
   is the permission model's, not this module's.

## Consequences

- `packages/authority/src/workflow/resource.ts` (`checkParticipants`) is the check the endpoint of FRW-025
  will call. Its tests are `packages/authority/test/frw-025.resource.test.ts`.
- The `name` member is accepted, but the workflow record of `lifecycle.ts` does not yet store it. The
  resource (FRW-025) will add it.
- The published schema of the resource (FRW-025 criterion 1) and the schema statement of FRW-026
  criterion 4 remain open until the schema is generated into `spec/`.

## What remains open

- The type and range of `order` (decision 1) and the maximum number of participants.
- Whether `name` is required for a participant who is not a `sub`-bound host user.
- Whether an owner may give a participant a `sub` before the host has one (the binding needs `sub`,
  FRW-038).

## Save-time pattern check (FRW-018 criterion 1; round 3, 2026-11-13)

Status: proposed (a maintainer's proposal from lane FRW-014-026; not reviewed by the owner).

Decisions taken where 12 and ADR 0030 are silent:

7. The save-time check is a separate function, `checkDocumentPatterns` (`packages/rules/src/definitions.ts`),
   not a change to `compileDocument`. It reports only the three pattern codes (`pattern_syntax`,
   `pattern_unsupported`, `pattern_limit`) of every `validation.pattern` and every `matches` pattern, each
   with its construct and an absolute JSON Pointer. Other problems stay with the send checklist and the
   rule builder (12 section 6.2), so an existing refusal does not change.
8. The caller decides what a report means. The save path (the authority's commit, in its package) must call
   it for FRW-018 criterion 1 to be met; until it does, the criterion stays unticked.
9. `fieldFrom` of `packages/rules/src/document.ts` is exported so that the check reads a field's attributes
   the same way as `readDocument`.

Open: whether the document service or the authority refuses a commit that carries a pattern problem, or
only the send (decision 8); the message of `validation.pattern` (ADR 0030, open item 3).

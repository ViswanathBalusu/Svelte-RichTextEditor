# ADR 0227: Decisions in lane EXP-079-091 (export, round 2)

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-13. Written by the developer of lane EXP-079-091 (round 2 of the run). The project owner has not reviewed it. Where a story left a choice open, the choice below is the spec's default or is marked as open.

**Context.** Lane EXP-079-091 covers the export stories EXP-079 to EXP-091 in `docs/stories/EXP-export.md`. Stories EXP-087 and EXP-088 were handled in round 1 and are not repeated. Most of the rest need the export worker, the signing service, a Word or LuaLaTeX run, or a model change owned by another package. This record covers the choices and gaps the lane met.

## Decisions

1. **EXP-083 criterion 3 is partly built, and stays unticked.** Paragraphs with an `ins` or `del` paragraph mark are marked in markup mode: each text run of the paragraph is marked as if it carried the mark (`blockMarks` in `packages/export-latex/src/inline.ts`, `runMarks`). A run's own mark of the same type is not added twice. Tables, table rows and atoms (images, fields, equations) with a suggestion are still printed unmarked, so the criterion is open. The choice of marking a whole paragraph by wrapping its runs (not by a margin bar) is the lane's; the owner may prefer a margin bar, and the criterion does not say.
   - The existing test `packages/export-latex/src/suggestions.markup.test.ts`, 'writes insertions in the insertion colour and underlined, deletions in the deletion colour and struck through', asserts one deletion colour macro. After this change the struck paragraph in its sample is marked too, so the count is two. That test was not written by this lane and is left as it is; its owner should update the expected count, or the paragraph case should be treated as out of scope for that test.

2. **EXP-091 criterion 2 cannot be met for inline images as written.** The CDM `image` node (`packages/model/src/definition/cdm1.ts`, the node named `image`) has the attributes `asset`, `width`, `height`, `alt`, `preserved`, `origin` and `ext`. It has no `align`. The DOCX writer reads `align` only from a figure (`figureXml` in `packages/export-docx/src/writer.ts`). Two readings are open: (a) the criterion means figures only, which the tests already cover (`exp-091-figures.test.ts`), or (b) inline images need an `align` attribute, which is a model change in another package and a joint decision. The lane does not change the model and does not tick the criterion.

3. **EXP-090 criterion 7 (OOXML schema validation in CI) is not met.** No schema validation exists in the repository: a search of `packages/export-docx`, `packages/export-latex` and `tests` finds none. The XSD files, the validator and the CI step are not built. The criterion stays open.

4. **EXP-084 (comments in the margin) is not built.** `packages/export-latex` has no margin writer, and the converter does not read the `comments` option, so none of its criteria is ticked. The mapped ranges come from the document service (SYN-032); the lane has not built the margin package.

5. **EXP-081 criterion 4 (an unverifiable mark) is not decided.** The converter reads only `ins` and `del` marks (`suggestions.ts`, `hasSuggestion`, `suggestionHides`). The spec does not say which marks the exporter cannot verify, or what the diagnostic names. Until the owner or the spec gives that definition, the criterion stays unticked. The lane does not invent a rule for it.

6. **EXP-085 criterion 4 (licence manifest in the archive) stays open.** The licence manifest of the packages sits in the export worker image (EXP-073, `services/export-worker`), outside `packages/export-latex`. The archive writer does not read it.

7. **Not decided here:** the marks of a table row or table in markup mode (decision 1), the margin rendering of comments (decision 4), and the mark that marks an inline image (decision 2). Each is a spec question or a story of its own.

## Round 3 (2026-11-13)

8. **EXP-087 criterion 5 is ticked for headings, lists, tables and paragraphs.** The reading of "visible text" is the reader's: tags become spaces, entities are decoded, and list labels (•, 1., 1.1.) count, because a reader sees them. The spec does not define it; this is the lane's reading. Footnotes, pictures and equations are not in that comparison. Test: packages/export-latex/src/html-writer.visible-text.test.ts.

9. **EXP-089 (classification label) is not built.** The converter has no input for the document's classification label; the label reaches only the document service (services/document-service/src/control-plane/policy-hook.ts, handlers.ts). Placing the marking on every page needs a change to the house class, and the placement is open in the spec. Nothing ticked.

10. **EXP-079 (two compiles before sealing) is not built.** No two-worker comparison exists in the repository, and the sealing step belongs to FRW and the signing service. Nothing ticked.

11. **EXP-082 and EXP-086 criterion 4 stay with the service.** The converter's halves are already tested (round 1 and 2). The request refusal, the job states and the RFC 9457 problem details are not in this package.

12. **EXP-088 criterion 2 (rendered text equals typed text) stays open.** The workspace has no Markdown renderer, so the rendered text cannot be read back. No renderer was added.

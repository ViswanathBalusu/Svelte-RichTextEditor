Status: proposed. Date: 2026-11-13.

## Context

Lane V-EXP-065-080 closes the criteria of EXP-065 to EXP-080 that were blocked on a container, a browser, another package or an owner decision. The stories EXP-066, EXP-068, EXP-075, EXP-078 and EXP-079 were handled in earlier rounds and are not repeated here.

## Decisions taken in this round

1. EXP-065 criterion 4 (codes published with the class). The catalogue `CODES` is written to `spec/export/diagnostic-codes-1.json` with `classVersion`. The file is generated by `services/export-worker/scripts/write-diagnostic-codes.ts`, and a test fails on drift. Spec default used: the artifact lives in `spec/`, as the other machine-readable artifacts do. Not decided here: the root `pnpm generate` does not call the script yet, because that needs a `generate` script in `services/export-worker/package.json`. The main session must add it.

## Left open, not decided

- EXP-067 criterion 4 (a worker refuses a bundle the service did not queue): the spec does not say how a worker knows a bundle is the converter's. Needs an owner decision (see Open questions in the story file). Not built.
- EXP-070 criteria 2 and 4, and EXP-077 criteria 1 to 5: need a second host, an orchestrator and a deployed scaling controller. Not available on this machine.
- EXP-069: the choice between gVisor and Firecracker needs measurements of both on the reference machine. The choice is the owner's.
- EXP-072 criteria 1 and 4: the worker has no write-only upload location (outputs return on standard output). Needs a change to the worker's output path and the service.
- EXP-073 criteria 1, 3 and 4: the image is the full TeX Live scheme, not the curated list, and no licence manifest or release exists.
- EXP-065 criteria 2 and 3: the converter does not emit most approximation warnings (see the story Notes); criterion 3 is the editor's.

## Round 4 (2026-11-13)

2. EXP-079 (compare two compiles before sealing). Decision taken: the pair rule lives in `services/export-worker/src/exp-079-pair.ts`. An equal pair is the only way to get a value that `acceptForSealing` accepts. Spec default used for a failed or unequal pair: no PDF is returned, and a diagnostic string says why, so the job can fail with it. Not decided: whether the two compiles run on two hosts or two sandboxes on one host. This round runs two sandboxes on one host, which proves the sandbox and comparison halves only. The owner or the main session must say what "independent workers" means for deployment (ADR 0116 and 09 §9.3 item 8). Not built: the job wiring (criteria 2 to 4), the hash record in the job result, the operator alert, and the charge against the tenant's compile minutes.
3. Story EXP-079 closed only partly; no criterion is ticked. The Podman test is `services/export-worker/src/exp-079-pair.podman.test.ts`.
4. Open for the owner, not decided here: what "image digest" means for EXP-073 criterion 2 (see ADR 0116 decision 4). This round did not change `imageDigest`.

Status: proposed. Owner decisions are the owner's; the spec default is used where the spec leaves a question open, and the default is named below.

Stories in the lane's range, not handled in round 1 (SYN-047, 037, 039, 040, 042, 043, 044, 045, 046, 049, 050), and not blocked by another lane: SYN-038 (phase 1b, spike) and SYN-041 (phase 4). SYN-051, SYN-052 and SYN-053 have no unticked criterion. SYN-048 was merged into BE-080 and has no story of its own in the file.

## SYN-041: coalescing (round 2)

- The wire form is already decided by the protocol: one `sync.commit` with a `commits` array (`packages/protocol/src/bridge/room-bridge.ts`). Default used: no new message.
- The window is held by `CommitCoalescer` (`packages/protocol/src/bridge/coalesce.ts`). The spec target is 30 to 50 ms; the tests use 40 ms. No window is set as a product default in this round.
- Flush before acknowledgements, rejections and closes on the same channel (the SYN-015 ordering). Default used; this answers question 2 of ADR 0270.
- Subscriber threshold ("many"): not set. The coalescer takes a `coalesce` flag from the host, so the threshold is the host's to choose. This is an open question and an owner or document-service decision; no number is invented.
- Not done: wiring the coalescer into the room's delivery path in `services/document-service`. That needs the host's timer and subscriber count. Criterion 1 of SYN-041 stays unticked for that reason.

## SYN-038: calibration spike (not started)

The spike needs recorded or simulated editing traces, a reproducible catch-up cost model and a rolling-hash cost on documents of 1, 10 and 100 pages. None of those was built in this round, so no criterion was ticked and no figure was reported. The next step is a spike script under `packages/authority/scripts/`, in the style of `projection-cost.ts`, that prints the machine it ran on and one row per setting.

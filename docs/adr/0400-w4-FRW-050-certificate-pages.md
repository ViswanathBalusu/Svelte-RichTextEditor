# 0400. The certificate pages of the final export

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-18. Written while building FRW-050 (lane w4). 12 §8.2 step 1 and 09 §4 say the certificate is generated LaTeX built from the evidence through the typed emitters, and leave its form, its wording beyond the fixed sentences and the way the facts reach the worker open. The owner has not seen this record.

1. **The certificate is CDM blocks, not TeX.** `appendCertificate` (`packages/export-latex/src/certificate.ts`) appends plain paragraphs to the body of the envelope, before anything else reads it. The first has `pageBreakBefore` and a bold run; the others hold one fact each. They go through the converter's own block and text emitters, so no string is interpolated into TeX by hand (09 §4), the pages are tagged and numbered like the body, and the body's blocks are untouched (the test compares the TeX of every body block with and without the certificate). The certificate starts on a new page, so the pagination of the body does not change. Headings are bold paragraphs, not heading styles: a heading style would need a style definition in the document, which a certificate must not add to the owner's styles. Whether the certificate should have a heading structure for the PDF's outline is open (FRW-050 criterion 6).
2. **The facts come from the service, never from the client.** The final export job carries them in the payload member `certificate`, written only by `startFinalExport` (ADR 0399), checked and ordered by `buildCertificateFacts`. A client's `options.certificate: true` on an ordinary export is refused by the worker (`option_unsupported`) because the payload carries no facts; the worker also refuses facts that are not the shape the service writes (`readCertificateFacts`, a job failure `internal`). The queue proof (EXP-067) covers the payload.
3. **What is printed.** Title; the fixed sentences of `CERTIFICATE_STATEMENT` (FRW-058, not approved by counsel, FRW-087); the document and workflow identifiers; `sentSeq`; `completedSeq`; the chain head at `completedSeq` (the hash of that commit; for a document with no commit the hash of its canonical content, as at the send); then, for each participant (all of them, by `name` or identifier), one line for each of: viewed, consent accepted, signed (one for each signing), completed, with the server time as `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC`. The time zone of the workflow is not applied: the certificate states server times in UTC.
4. **Network identity.** The address and the user agent are printed on an event only when the row holds them and the tenant's capture setting is on at generation (`EvidenceLedger.settings`, FRW-042). With it off they are not in the facts at all. The composition does not yet change the setting (the administration route of FRW-042 is not built), so the live switch is tested through the in-memory ledger's setting function.
5. **Not done.** No PDF of the certificate has been compiled, tagged-checked or read by a screen reader here (the local compile tests skip on this machine for want of the font directory): that the pages compile with hostile names, are tagged like the body and pass veraPDF (criteria 4 and 6) is open. The wording of the evidence lines ("Viewed the document", "Accepted the consent text") is the lane's and has not been seen by counsel.

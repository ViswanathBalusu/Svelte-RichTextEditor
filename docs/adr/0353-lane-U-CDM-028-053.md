# ADR 0353: Open decisions of lane U-CDM-028-053 (round 3)

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-13. Not reviewed by the owner or by the owners of FE and EXP.

## Context

Round 3 of lane U-CDM-028-053 closed the open layout items of CDM-043 (ADR 0032, section 3). Two of them were product behaviour that ADR 0032 already decides: cells and stories follow the gap rule, and section breaks are transparent. One is still open in ADR 0032 and this lane has not decided it.

## Decision

1. **Gap rule in containers and across section breaks.** Implemented as ADR 0032 section 3 states (no new decision): the gap rule runs once over the whole body, across section breaks, and over paragraphs in table cells and stories. Tested in `packages/layout/src/cdm-043-containers.test.ts`.

2. **Spacing before at the top of a page (open, not decided here).** ADR 0032 leaves open whether `spaceBefore` is dropped at the top of a page or column. The layout keeps it, which is the current behaviour, and the rule is unchanged. The owner of pagination (02 §9.4) and the owners of FE and EXP decide. Spec default: keep it until they decide.

3. **Footnote paragraphs.** A footnote holds one paragraph in version 1 (CDM-001), so no gap rule is applied to it. If footnotes ever hold several paragraphs, the rule must be extended. Recorded here, not decided.

## Consequences

- CDM-043 criterion 5 (the full fixture run in both engines) stays unticked: the LaTeX class has not run `paragraph-spacing.json` and `line-spacing.json`.
- CDM-044 criterion 4 and CDM-045 criterion 3 depend on the same LaTeX comparison and stay unticked.

## Round 5 (2026-11-13): layout side of CDM-043, CDM-044 and CDM-045

Status: proposed. Round 5 ran the shared fixtures of `packages/model/fixtures/semantics` through the layout engine, in Node.js, and recorded what the layout does not yet do. Nothing here changes the spec.

1. **Centre tab rounding (decided by the spec, applied).** 03 §7 and the model's `placeTab` round the half-width of a centred segment down to whole twips. `placeAfterTab` in `packages/layout/src/lines.ts` now does the same, in points (`floor(width * 10) / 20`). Tests: `packages/layout/src/cdm-044-tabs.test.ts` and the existing `lines-aligned-tabs.test.ts`, which still pass.

2. **Layout `multiple` heights are not rounded to whole twips (open, not changed).** The model rounds half up to whole twips (`lineHeight`); the layout keeps the exact product (a 1.15 factor on a 253-twip run gives 290.95 twips against 291). The test accepts a difference of up to half a twip. Rounding would move the digests of `layout.universal.test.ts`. The owner of the layout (FE) decides. Spec default: keep the exact value until FE decides.

3. **Hanging-indent stop missing in the layout (open, not changed).** 03 §7 and `nextTabStop` treat a hanging indent's left indent as a stop that beats the default stops at a tie and does not clear them. `breakParagraph` has no such stop. The shared fixtures agree with the layout only because their hanging indents are multiples of `defaultTabStop`. With a left indent of 500 twips, a hanging first line and a tab from 100 twips, the model gives 500 and the layout gives 720. The owner of FE decides whether to add the stop. Spec default: add it as the model does.

4. **Body end under a footer (not checked).** `paginate` does not expose the body bottom of a page, so the layout test in `packages/layout/src/cdm-045-header-growth.test.ts` checks the body start only.

5. **Slots of headers and footers (not checked by the layout test).** The `slots` cases of `header-growth.json` are not run through the layout.

Criteria still unticked: CDM-043 (5), CDM-044 (4) and CDM-045 (3), each for the LaTeX class side. TeX Live is installed at `E:\texlive\2025`, so the next step is a compile harness that reads the body start and the line heights from the PDF.

## Round 6 (2026-11-13): CDM-042 labels, open findings

Status: proposed. Round 6 read the label paths of the writers. Nothing here changes the spec or the code.

1. **DOCX lists use Word's own numbering (open, not changed).** `packages/export-docx/src/writer-styles.ts` (`w:numFmt`, `w:lvlText`) writes numbering definitions and `packages/export-docx/src/writer.ts` writes `w:numPr` on list paragraphs. Word then computes the list labels with its own counters, not the output of `computeLabels`. CDM-042 criterion 6 asks that the writers print that output as literal text and keep no counters of their own, so it stays unticked. Changing it would drop Word's editable lists, which is a design decision for the owner of EXP-DOCX and the owner of the writer. Spec default: keep the native numbering until they decide. Equation, sequence and footnote labels in the DOCX writer already use `computeLabels` (`writer.ts` `labels.equations`, `labels.sequences`).

2. **Page numbers and a footnote fallback in the layout (allowed, noted).** `packages/layout/src/flow.ts` (`formatNumber`, `toRoman`, `toLetters`) formats page numbers (`paginate.ts`, `notes.ts`), which 03 §7 and CDM-042 leave out of `computeLabels`. The same function is the fallback for a footnote mark that `footnoteLabels` lacks (`flow.ts` near `ctx.footnoteLabels.get(id) ?? formatNumber(...)`). No list label is computed there: list labels come from `computeLabels` (`flow.ts` near `computeLabels(doc ...)`).

3. **Criterion 1 of CDM-042 (not ticked).** The LaTeX converter, the HTML and Markdown writers, the layout and the DOCX writer all take list labels from `computeLabels`. The DOCX numbering in item 1 is the open part, so the criterion is not ticked on the static reading alone.

4. **CDM-042 criterion 7 (not run).** Needs the reference machine named in 13 §9. The figures in the Notes were measured on another machine and do not count.

## Round 7 (2026-11-13): survey of CDM-028 to CDM-053

Status: proposed. Round 7 surveyed the range for criteria that need PostgreSQL, object storage, a container or a browser. None of the unticked criteria in the range names one of them, so no new container or browser test was written this round. The rest are blocked as follows.

1. **CDM-050 criterion 2 (commit stamp, not ticked).** The room in `packages/authority/src/room/` stamps the attribution (user, session, time), and the `cdm` version and engine identifier come from the room's own configuration, not from `@docedit/model`. Open: whether the model should export a stamp helper that the room calls, or whether the room alone stamps. Spec default (03 §11.3, 08 §3): the service stamps, so the room owns it. No code was added, because a helper nothing calls would not prove the criterion.
2. **CDM-050 criteria 1, 3, 4 and 6 (not ticked).** They need a version n-1 and a migration, which do not exist (CDM-049), or a release process (FND-012).
3. **CDM-042 criterion 1 and 6 (not ticked).** Item 1 of round 6 above (DOCX native numbering) stands. Changing the DOCX writer to print `computeLabels` output as literal text would change lossless round-trip behaviour (A2), so it is an owner decision. It is listed in needsFromOthers.
4. **CDM-049, CDM-052, CDM-053 and the other unticked criteria in the range** wait for version 2 content, the owners of FE, EXP or FRW, or the reference machine (13 §9). Their Notes already say so.

Evidence this round: `pnpm vitest run --project node packages/model/src/definition/supported.test.ts` passed (3 tests, Node.js, Windows, 2026-11-13).
